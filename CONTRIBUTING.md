# Contributing to MCCCD District ITS projects

Follow [central review governance](https://github.com/mcccd-IT-Operations/.github/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md), [documentation standards](https://github.com/mcccd-IT-Operations/.github/blob/main/docs/code-documentation.md) and [routing guidance](https://github.com/mcccd-IT-Operations/.github/blob/main/docs/review-routing.md). Project-specific README, validation, recovery and approval records provide local context.

Create a review branch and draft pull request. Explain what changed and why; include opening code notes and continued section comments. Do not commit credentials, operator configuration, unredacted logs or account screenshots.

All five pillars assess impact. Obtain every affected-pillar/additional required approval, acknowledged reasoned Not applicable decisions, technical peer review by someone other than the author and final CyberSecurity approval by someone distinct from author and peer. Final approvals reference the final commit; changes require renewed review.

Templates and downloads are not approval. Repository administrators must separately configure and verify access, merge rules and checks; maintainer role-by-role verification remains necessary. Production needs its own approved artifact and applicable deployment/change authorization.

For a new project, copy the [project starter files](https://github.com/mcccd-IT-Operations/.github/blob/main/templates/project/) and complete [template adoption](https://github.com/mcccd-IT-Operations/.github/blob/main/docs/template-adoption.md). Keep project code and operational evidence in its project repository.
